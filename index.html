How We Source

Every car has a story
we can prove.

Private Acquisitions

We work directly with ultra-high-net-worth individuals who prefer discretion over public listings. Every acquisition is handled with absolute confidentiality — no auction houses, no public exposure.

Dubai Impound Recovery

Through a special arrangement with Dubai authorities, we acquire supercars abandoned or seized in impound lots. Original owners are traced and compensated where possible. Each car is fully reconditioned before sale.

Full Provenance

Every vehicle comes with complete documentation — service history, ownership chain, condition reports, and certification. No unknowns, no surprises. Just verified, investment-grade automobiles.
