Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

1. Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us through the enquiry form: your name, email address and the details of your message. Nothing is gathered automatically while you browse.

2. How we use it

  • To answer your questions about our vehicles and services.
  • To arrange viewings, test drives and follow-up conversations.
  • To keep you posted on vehicles you've shown interest in.

We never sell, rent or share your details with third parties for marketing.

3. How we protect it

Anything you send is treated as confidential and seen only by our sales team. We apply sensible administrative and technical safeguards to keep it that way.

4. How long we keep it

We hold your enquiry only as long as it takes to deal with your request, then delete it securely.

5. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent to be contacted at any time — just email enquiries@obsidianmotors.example.
