Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you choose to provide through our enquiry form, including your name, email address and the details of your message. We do not collect any information automatically while you browse this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries about our vehicles and services.
  • To arrange viewings, test drives or follow-up conversations.
  • To keep you informed about vehicles you have expressed interest in.

We will never sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How We Protect Your Information

Any information you submit is treated as confidential and is only accessible to our sales team. We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data.

4. Data Retention

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to deal with your request, after which they are securely deleted.

5. Your Rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to be contacted at any time by emailing enquiries@obsidianmotors.example.
