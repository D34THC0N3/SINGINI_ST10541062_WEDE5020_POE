Our Story

We don't sell cars.
We curate legacies.

[ About hero image ]

Obsidian Motors was founded on a single conviction: the world's most extraordinary automobiles deserve an intermediary as exceptional as the machines themselves.

We are not a traditional dealership. There is no showroom floor buzzing with salespeople, no rows of inventory parked under fluorescent lights. Instead, there is a private network — quiet, discreet, and meticulously maintained — connecting the rarest vehicles on Earth with collectors who understand their true value.

01

Private Acquisitions

Our primary channel serves ultra-high-net-worth individuals who wish to part with extraordinary vehicles without public exposure. No auction houses, no online listings, no spectacle. We handle every aspect of the transaction with the discretion of a private banker — valuation, documentation, logistics, and transfer.

Many of these vehicles have never been publicly listed. Some have never left the private garages they were delivered to. When they do move, they move through us.

[ Private acquisition ]
02

Dubai Recovery Programme

Through a special arrangement with Dubai authorities, Obsidian Motors holds the exclusive permit to acquire supercars and hypercars that have been abandoned or seized in municipal impound lots. These are Bugattis, Paganis, Lamborghinis, and one-offs simply left behind by owners who moved on.

Every recovered vehicle undergoes a rigorous process: we trace the original owner and offer fair compensation wherever possible. The car is then fully inspected, reconditioned by certified specialists, and documented from bumper to bumper before it enters our collection.
