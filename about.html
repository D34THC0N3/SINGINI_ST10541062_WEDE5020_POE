Our story

How rare cars
quietly change hands.

Obsidian Motors runs on one belief: the rarest cars on Earth deserve someone who treats them as more than stock to shift.

We're not a dealership. No showroom floor full of salespeople, no rows of cars under fluorescent light, no monthly targets. What we have instead is a private network — quiet, careful, built over years — that connects exceptional machines with the people who understand what they're actually worth.

01

Private acquisitions

Most of what we handle never reaches a listing. A collector wants to move something rare without the noise of an auction or the exposure of a public sale, so they call us. We take care of the valuation, the paperwork, the logistics, and the transfer — start to finish, without the story getting out.

Some of these cars have never been photographed for the public. A few have never left the garage they were first delivered to. When they do move, they move through us.

02

Dubai recovery programme

We hold a permit that's genuinely hard to come by: the right to acquire supercars left behind in Dubai's municipal impound lots. Bugattis, Paganis, Lamborghinis — cars abandoned by owners who moved on and never came back for them.

Where we can trace the original owner, we do, and we offer fair compensation. Then the car is stripped down, inspected, brought back to condition by specialists, and documented properly before it earns a place in the collection.

Our promise

What you can count on.

Absolute discretion

Every deal stays between us. Who's selling, what changed hands, what's in the collection — none of it leaves the room.

Complete documentation

Service history, ownership chain, condition reports, third-party certification. Every car arrives with its paperwork in order.

Investment-grade quality

We're picky about what we take on. If a car doesn't pass inspection, it doesn't get our name on it.

Global reach

Our network runs across six continents — private collectors, a few royal households, people who notice when something rare surfaces.
